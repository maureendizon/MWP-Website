Physician-supervised weight loss from Dr. Minnie Cruz-Tolentino, a board-certified obesity medicine doctor, for patients across Loudoun County.

Book an appointment Call 703-777-9355

Weight loss that treats the cause

Obesity is a chronic medical condition, not a lack of willpower. Genetics, hormones, sleep, stress, other health conditions and some medications can all make it harder to lose weight and keep it off.

Medical weight loss looks at those causes. Instead of a one-size-fits-all diet, you get a plan built around your health history and goals, with a physician following your progress over time.

How we help

  • A full medical evaluation of your health history, current medications and the factors that affect your weight.
  • A personalized care plan based on evidence-based treatment.
  • Nutritional education so you understand what works for your body.
  • Ongoing follow-up to adjust your plan as you progress.

Our goal is sustainable results, not quick fixes.

Why see an obesity medicine specialist

Dr. Cruz-Tolentino is board-certified by the American Board of Obesity Medicine and the American Board of Family Medicine. That combination means your weight is treated as part of your overall health, alongside conditions such as high blood pressure, diabetes and hormonal imbalance.

In person or by telemedicine

See us at our office in Leesburg, or have your follow-up visits by telemedicine from home. Patients come to us from across Loudoun County and surrounding areas.
