What is lipedema?

Lipedema is a chronic condition in which fat builds up in an abnormal way, usually in the legs and sometimes the arms. It affects mostly women and often starts or gets worse around hormonal changes such as puberty, pregnancy or menopause.

Lipedema is often mistaken for ordinary weight gain or for lymphedema. Many people live with it for years before getting a diagnosis, and the fat it causes usually doesn’t respond to diet and exercise alone.

Common signs

  • Fat build-up on both legs, or both arms, that looks out of proportion with the rest of the body
  • Feet and hands that are not affected, sometimes with a “cuff” of tissue at the ankles or wrists
  • Legs that feel painful, tender or heavy
  • Bruising easily
  • Little change in the affected areas despite weight loss

How we help

Dr. Cruz-Tolentino starts with a careful evaluation to confirm the diagnosis and rule out other causes of swelling. From there, she creates a personalized plan to manage your symptoms, support your overall health and help you stay active.

Because lipedema often appears alongside weight and hormonal concerns, care is coordinated with our medical weight loss and hormone therapy services when that helps.
