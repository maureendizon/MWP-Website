Root causes, not quick fixes.

Dr. Cruz-Tolentino is double board-certified in Family Medicine and Obesity Medicine, with over 19 years of experience helping patients achieve lasting wellness. As the founder of My Wellness Physicians, she specializes in lipedema and lymphedema diagnosis and management, medical weight loss, hormone replacement therapy and preventive health care for men and women across Loudoun County and surrounding areas.

She pairs her medical background with a compassionate, patient-centered approach. Her goal is to guide every patient toward sustainable results through evidence-based treatment, nutritional education and a personalized care plan, because true transformation begins when you understand your body.

Clinical specialties.

Each specialty draws on double board certification in Family Medicine and Obesity Medicine and over 19 years of experience, with care that addresses the root causes of weight gain, hormonal imbalance and chronic health concerns.

  • Lipedema

    Diagnosis and ongoing management of lipedema, a chronic condition that causes painful fat build-up, usually in the legs and arms, and is often mistaken for ordinary weight gain.

    Learn more about lipedema treatment

  • Lymphedema

    Diagnosis and management of lymphedema, swelling caused by a build-up of lymph fluid when the lymphatic system is blocked or damaged.

    Learn more about lymphedema treatment

  • Weight loss & obesity management

    Physician-supervised weight loss from a board-certified obesity medicine doctor, with nutritional education and a plan built around your health history.

    Learn more about weight loss and obesity management

  • Hormone therapy for men & women

    Hormone replacement therapy (HRT) for men and women dealing with hormonal imbalance, guided by your symptoms and health history.

    Book a visit for hormone therapy

  • Primary care

    Comprehensive primary care for men and women: preventive check-ups, early detection and ongoing management of chronic conditions.

    Book a visit for primary care

  • Wellness consultation

    A one-on-one visit to review your health, goals and concerns, and map out a personalized plan for feeling your best.

    Book a visit for a wellness consultation

  • IV therapy & peptides

    IV therapy and peptide therapy, offered when appropriate as part of a personalized, physician-guided plan.

    Book a visit for IV therapy and peptides

How do I book an appointment?

Book online at any time, call 703-777-9355 during office hours, or email info@mywellnessphysicians.com.

Do you offer telemedicine visits?

Yes. You can see Dr. Cruz-Tolentino in our Leesburg office or by telemedicine from home.

What conditions do you treat?

Lipedema, lymphedema, weight loss and obesity, hormone imbalance in men and women, and everyday primary care. We also offer wellness consultations, and IV therapy and peptides.

What areas do you serve?

Patients come to us from Leesburg, across Loudoun County and throughout Northern Virginia. Telemedicine visits make it easy to see us from further away.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your medical records, a list of your current medications, and any questions you’d like to ask.
